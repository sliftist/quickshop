# Quick Shop Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Quick Shop is a browser extension that adds a fast search and cart window to zehrs.ca pages. This policy explains what data it handles.

## Summary

Quick Shop does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data. It has no servers, no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising.

## What the extension does

- It runs only on zehrs.ca pages.
- When you search or change a quantity, it sends the same requests the Zehrs website itself sends, to the Zehrs / PC Express API (`api.pcexpress.ca`), using your existing session on the site. Those requests go directly from your browser to Zehrs, exactly as if you had used the website. Their handling is covered by the Zehrs and PC Express privacy policies.
- It reads the store and cart identifiers that the Zehrs site already keeps in your browser, so that searches and cart changes apply to your selected store and your own cart.

## Data stored on your device

Quick Shop keeps a small cache of product package sizes (for example, "450 g") in the zehrs.ca site's local storage in your browser. This is used to show cart weights and cost per 100 g. It contains product information only, never personal information, and it never leaves your device. Clearing your browser's site data for zehrs.ca removes it.

## Data sent to the developer or third parties

None. Quick Shop never sends any data to the developer or to any party other than Zehrs / PC Express, and only sends to them the requests described above.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same location, with a new "Last updated" date.
